OpenHouse Lead Capture

A fast, friendly open-house sign-in kiosk for real estate agents — capture every visitor and send them straight to your CRM.

What it does

Set up an iPad, iPhone, or Android phone or tablet at your open house. Visitors sign in with their name and contact info, answer your custom questions, and you get the lead in Follow Up Boss automatically — so you can follow up while the visit is still fresh.

Features

Instant CRM sync

Sign-ins are sent to your Follow Up Boss account as new contacts and open-house events.

Works offline

No Wi-Fi at the property? Sign-ins are stored on the device and synced automatically once you're back online.

Kiosk mode

The screen stays awake, and Guided Access locks the device to the sign-in flow for a hands-off kiosk.

Your branding

Add your own logo, welcome message, and property details for a polished, on-brand sign-in.

Custom questions

Ask the questions that matter — financing, working with an agent, and more — with simple yes/no or text answers.

Private & secure

Your API key is stored in the device Keychain and Settings is protected by Face ID / Touch ID.
