Privacy Policy

OpenHouse Lead Capture
Effective date: June 16, 2026 · Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the OpenHouse Lead Capture app ("the App," "we," "us") handles information. The App is published by Savannah's Lamps and Plants, is available for iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play), and is used by real estate agents to run an open-house sign-in kiosk that collects visitor contact details and keeps them on the agent's device.

The App has no servers and does not send Visitor information anywhere. Where data practices differ between the iOS and Android versions, this policy says so explicitly.

1. Two kinds of users

This policy covers two different roles:

  • The Agent (operator): the real estate professional who installs the App, configures it, and operates the kiosk at an open house.
  • The Visitor (data subject): a person who signs in at the open house using the kiosk.

The Agent is responsible for displaying or making this policy available to Visitors and for handling Visitor information lawfully, including any copies the Agent exports from the App.

2. Information the App handles

Information Visitors provide

When a Visitor signs in, the App stores the information entered on the form, which may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Answers to custom questions configured by the Agent (for example, whether the Visitor is working with an agent, or free-text responses)
  • Booth activity results, if the Agent turns on an optional booth game: the prize won in "Spin the Wheel" or the price guess entered in "Guess the Price"

Information the Agent provides

The Agent enters configuration data, including:

  • Property details (address, city, state, ZIP, MLS number)
  • A welcome message, custom questions, a logo, and an app color
  • Booth activity settings: Spin the Wheel prize options (with odds, stock counts and optional redemption codes) or Guess the Price listing photos

Information accessed on the device

  • Location (when in use): With the Agent's permission, the App can read the device's location on the device only to auto-fill the property address during setup. Location is used transiently to look up a nearby address (via the operating system's geocoder) and is not stored or transmitted by the App as location data.
  • Photos: When the Agent chooses a logo or Guess the Price listing photos, the App reads only the images the Agent selects in the system photo picker and saves copies inside the App.
  • Biometric / device-lock authentication: Used to unlock the App's Settings screen. This uses Face ID / Touch ID on iOS and the device biometric prompt (fingerprint or face unlock, with a PIN / pattern / passcode fallback) on Android. Authentication is performed entirely by the operating system on the device; the App never receives or stores biometric data.

The App contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. We do not collect advertising identifiers, and we do not track Visitors or Agents across other companies' apps or websites.

3. How information is used

  • Visitor information is stored on the device so the Agent can review it and follow up with the Visitor.
  • Booth activity results let the Agent award prizes and see Visitors' price guesses. The App also keeps a prize-redemption log on the device so the Agent can mark prizes as handed out.
  • Agent configuration is used to operate the kiosk.
  • Location is used only to help the Agent fill in the property address during setup.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the open-house sign-in function.

4. How information is stored and shared

  • On-device storage only: Visitor information, prize records, and listing photos are stored in the App's private storage on the Agent's device. The App does not transmit them to us or to any third party.
  • CSV export (initiated by the Agent): From the Settings screen, the Agent can export Visitor information as a CSV file and send it, using the device's standard share sheet, to a destination the Agent chooses (for example, email, a file app, or a CRM). The Agent controls that transfer and is responsible for the copy they share. The App deletes its own temporary export file the next time it starts and whenever contacts are deleted.
  • Device backups: Visitor information, prize records, the logo, and listing photos are excluded from device backups — iCloud and computer backups on iOS, and Google backup / device-to-device transfer on Android. On Android, only the Agent's appearance and app-color preferences are included in backups.
  • Apple iCloud (iOS only — configuration sync): On iOS, the App uses Apple's iCloud key-value store to sync the Agent's configuration (property details, welcome message, custom questions) across the Agent's own Apple devices. Visitor information is not synced through iCloud. The Android version does not sync anything across devices.
  • App stores / standard system services: Standard Apple App Store and Google Play services apply when the App is downloaded, installed, and run.

We do not sell or share personal information.

5. Security

  • The App's Settings screen — where Visitor information can be viewed, exported, or deleted — is protected by device authentication: Face ID / Touch ID on iOS and the biometric/device-lock prompt (with PIN / pattern / passcode fallback) on Android.
  • Settings is hidden from the recent-apps / App Switcher preview, and on Android it also cannot be screenshotted.
  • On iOS, Visitor information is stored with the operating system's complete data protection, which keeps it encrypted while the device is locked.
  • CSV exports neutralize spreadsheet formulas typed into form fields, so a Visitor's entry can't run as a formula when the file is opened.

No method of storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the App. Because Visitor information stays on the Agent's device, its security also depends on the Agent securing that device (for example, with a passcode) and any exported copies.

6. Data retention

  • On the device: Visitor information and prize records remain on the device until the Agent deletes them from Settings ("Delete All Contacts" / "Clear Redemption History"), or the App is deleted.
  • Exported copies: CSV files the Agent exports are retained wherever the Agent sent them, under the Agent's control.

The App does not operate a server or database that retains Visitor information beyond the device.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for use by adults in a professional real estate context and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA).

Because Visitor information is held only on the Agent's device (and in any copies the Agent exports), requests about Visitor data should be directed to the Agent operating the open house. We do not hold a copy. You may also contact us using the details below, and we will assist or direct the request appropriately.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected here.
