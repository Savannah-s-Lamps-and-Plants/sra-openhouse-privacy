OpenHouse Lead Capture

OpenHouse Lead Capture is an open-house sign-in kiosk for real estate agents. Visitors sign in on the device and their contact details are sent to the agent's Follow Up Boss CRM — with an offline mode that stores sign-ins and syncs them once the device is back online.

Frequently asked questions

How do I open Settings?
From the welcome screen, tap anywhere to reach the sign-in form, then press and hold the logo at the top for about 1.5 seconds. Settings is protected by Face ID / Touch ID.

How do I connect Follow Up Boss?
In Settings, enter your Follow Up Boss API key under "Follow Up Boss Settings." Sign-ins are then sent to your CRM as new contacts.

What happens if there's no internet?
Sign-ins are saved on the device and submitted automatically once a connection returns. You can review, edit, or export stored sign-ins from Settings > Offline Contacts.

How do I add my own logo or custom questions?
In Settings, use "Choose Logo Image" to set your branding, and the Questions section to add custom yes/no or text questions for visitors.

How do I lock the device down for a kiosk?
The screen stays awake while the app is open. For a fully locked-down kiosk, enable Guided Access in iOS Settings > Accessibility, then triple-click the side button to start it during your open house.
